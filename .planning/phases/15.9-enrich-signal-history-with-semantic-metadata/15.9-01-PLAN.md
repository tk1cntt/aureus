- `services/aureus-signal/engine/signals/ema.py`
- `services/aureus-signal/engine/signals/session.py`
- `services/aureus-signal/engine/signals/sweep.py`
- `services/aureus-signal/engine/signals/fvg.py`

- [ ] `structure.py`: (Dành cho CHOCH/BOS)

# Phase 15.8: Signal→Nautilus order-open delivery diagnosis & contract alignment — Context

**Gathered:** 2026-03-26
**Status:** Planned

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Khoanh vùng nguyên nhân vì sao `ORDER_OPEN` từ `aureus-signal` không tới được xử lý thực thi tại runtime Nautilus, và chốt contract runtime/payload để tránh mismatch theo symbol.

**In scope:**
- Luồng `aureus-signal` (`aureus:stream:{symbol}:orders`) → consumer tại `aureus-nautilus-node`/`aureus-nautilus-bridge`
- Contract symbol/stream (multi-symbol vs hardcoded stream)
- Contract payload `ORDER_OPEN` (field required, default/fallback policy, reject observability)

**Out of scope:**
- Refactor lớn strategy business rules
- Thiết kế lại toàn bộ execution architecture
- Tối ưu hiệu năng end-to-end
</domain>

<evidence>
## Observed Evidence (current code)

1. **Producer side (signal):** `services/aureus-signal/engine/orders.py` publish order events tới `aureus:stream:{symbol}:orders` (dynamic per symbol).
2. **Node execution consumer:** `services/aureus-nautilus-node/execution_client.py` currently poll fixed stream `aureus:stream:XAUUSD:orders` trong `connect/_poll_loop/_poll_orders_once`.
3. **Runtime wiring gap:** `services/aureus-nautilus-node/main.py` log warning rằng `AureusExecutionClient/SyncWorker` chưa được start trong main runtime.
4. **Bridge behavior:** `services/aureus-nautilus-bridge/main.py` scan wildcard `aureus:stream:*:orders`, nên bridge xử lý multi-symbol tốt hơn node execution client hiện tại.
5. **Payload strictness:** execution client validate nhiều trường (`trace_id`, `symbol`, `side`, `qty`, `sl/tp`, backfill/validator states), có nguy cơ reject nếu payload contract drift.
</evidence>

<gray_areas>
## Gray Areas & Decisions

### G1. Runtime execution owner
- **Question:** Ưu tiên node execution client hay bridge làm consumer chính cho `ORDER_OPEN`?
- **Default decision (proposed):** Giữ bridge là ingress chuẩn cho multi-symbol; node execution client chỉ chạy khi explicit mode/feature flag.

### G2. Stream subscription model
- **Question:** Node execution client chuyển từ fixed `XAUUSD` sang wildcard discovery ngay hay theo allowlist config?
- **Default decision (proposed):** Chuyển sang discovery theo pattern + symbol allowlist config để vừa multi-symbol vừa có guard.

### G3. Symbol contract
- **Question:** Nguồn symbol truth lấy từ payload hay derive từ stream key?
- **Default decision (proposed):** Payload `symbol` là source-of-truth; stream symbol chỉ dùng cross-check, mismatch => reject + metric.

### G4. ORDER_OPEN contract compatibility
- **Question:** Khi thiếu field mới (`entry_policy`, `expiry_policy`, `backfill_status`) xử lý hard reject hay compatibility fallback?
- **Default decision (proposed):** Áp dụng compatibility fallback có kiểm soát (emit warning metric), chỉ hard reject cho field critical (`trace_id`, `symbol`, `side`, `qty`, sl/tp khi required).

### G5. Observability contract
- **Question:** Chuẩn hóa diagnostics ở đâu?
- **Default decision (proposed):** Chuẩn hóa reason-code taxonomy dùng chung cho producer/consumer + counter metrics cho từng reject bucket.
</gray_areas>
